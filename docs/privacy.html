Privacy policy

Trackk UGC Stats · Last updated 25 November 2026

What this app is

Trackk UGC Stats is an internal tool used by Trackk to track the performance of videos that our UGC creators post on Instagram and YouTube. Only creators who work with Trackk are asked to connect their accounts.

What we access

  • Instagram (with your approval): your username, follower count, and insights for your posts and Reels, such as views, likes, comments, shares, saves, reach and average watch time.
  • YouTube (with your approval, read-only): your channel name, subscriber count, and analytics for your videos, such as views, likes, comments, shares, subscribers gained and average view duration.

We never post, comment, send messages, or change anything on your accounts. We don't access your password, direct messages or private personal information.

How we use it

The numbers are copied once a day into Trackk's private Notion workspace, so the Trackk team can review each creator's posting schedule and video performance. We don't sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone outside Trackk.

How it's stored

The access you approve is stored encrypted and is only used by the daily sync. Stats are kept for as long as you work with Trackk, or until you ask us to delete them.

Removing access

You can remove access at any time:

  • Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites
  • Google/YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions

To have your stats deleted, ask your Trackk manager.

Google API Services

Trackk UGC Stats' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
